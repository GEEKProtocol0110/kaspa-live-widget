Full description:

Keep an eye on Kaspa from your Android home screen.

Kaspa Live Widget brings market and network readings into a compact or expanded widget. Choose a neon dark or frosted light appearance, with an optional 24-hour price chart.

• KAS price in USD, EUR or GBP.
• DAA score, with observed blocks per second and network hashrate in the expanded widget.
• Compact and expanded layouts you can resize on your launcher.
• 15-, 30- or 60-minute background refresh options.
• Tap to request a fresh reading.
• Last saved values and their age remain visible when a source is unavailable.

Open the app, choose your settings, and select Save & add. You can also long-press your home screen, open Widgets, and drag a Kaspa widget into place. Launcher layouts and Android battery settings can affect sizing and refresh timing.

Public prices come from CoinGecko, with exchange fallbacks. Network readings use public Kaspa services and validated mainnet node fallbacks. Data can be delayed or unavailable. A KAS/USDT fallback is displayed as an approximation of USD.

Free and open source. No ads, wallet connection, seed phrase, purchases or trading features. This is an independent community utility from Geek Protocol, not an official Kaspa application. Market readings are informational and are not investment advice.

Requires Android 8.0 or later and an internet connection for updates.
