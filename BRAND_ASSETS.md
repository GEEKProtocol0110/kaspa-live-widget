# Kaspa mark

`kaspa_mark.xml` and `ic_launcher.xml` use the Kaspa media-kit icon path from `kaspa-melon-white.svg`, distributed by the community media-kit mirror https://github.com/thuun/kaspa-images (source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thuun/kaspa-images/master/logo/kaspa-melon-white.svg).

The original mint (#6FC7BA) and white colors, irregular coin outline and bent symbol are preserved. Empty source-canvas margins are cropped for small-widget legibility. The logo itself is not mirrored, redrawn or recolored. Neon/glass effects surround the logo rather than changing its geometry.

Brand reference: https://wiki.kaspa.org/en/faq (Brand section). Mirror states the files are free to use for the Kaspa community. Kaspa marks belong to their respective creators; this widget is a community project.
